/ 06 — questions

The straight answers.

What sellers actually ask before they migrate. If yours isn't here, DM the founder.

Why migrate if my dm2buy store works fine?

Two reasons sellers actually move:

One — they're scaling. They want Shopify's app ecosystem, international payments, or a more customizable storefront. dm2buy is great for getting started; it's not always where you finish.

Two — they want options. Moving to Shopify while the store is healthy is cleaner than doing it under pressure. Your catalog in Shopify is yours on better infrastructure.

Either reason is good enough. You don't need a crisis to want your store somewhere better.

How long does a migration take?

About 3 minutes per 100 products, including image downloads. A 500-SKU store finishes in roughly 15 minutes. You don't have to keep the tab open — the import keeps running and the products appear in Shopify admin when it's done.

What happens to my dm2buy store after I migrate?

Nothing. Shoprift only reads from dm2buy — it doesn't modify or delete anything. Your store keeps running until you decide to take it offline. Most sellers keep both live for a week or two while they redirect their domain.

Is my store data safe?

The only thing we keep is your migration job record (URL, timestamps, product count) for support purposes. Products and images are imported directly into your Shopify admin — nothing is stored on our servers long-term. Nothing is shared, nothing is sold.

Ownership is verified via Instagram before any extraction — you can't migrate a store you don't own.

What if some products don't extract cleanly?

The migration report lists every product that came through and every one that didn't, with the reason. If less than 95% of your catalog migrates due to an issue on our side, the migration is free — you don't pay for partial work.

Most failures are platform-side: an image expired on dm2buy's CDN, a product was removed from public view, etc. We document each one so you know what to re-add manually.

Does this only work with Shopify?

Yes — Shoprift imports directly into Shopify admin via the Shopify API. Products land in your catalog without any CSV step. No downloading, no separate import flow.

If you're moving to WooCommerce, Wix, or another platform, Shoprift isn't the right fit today. Shopify-first was a deliberate choice — it lets us go deeper on import quality instead of spreading thin across platforms.

Do you support source platforms other than dm2buy?

Right now, dm2buy is the only source — that's the rift we're solving first. We import directly into Shopify, so the destination is fixed for now.

Other Indian D2C platforms as sources are on the roadmap. DM the founder if you're on a different platform and want to move to Shopify.

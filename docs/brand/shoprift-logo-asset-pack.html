Every logo, ready to copy.

10 logo variants as production-ready SVG. Click Copy SVG to grab the code, or click Download to save the file. Everything you need to ship — no Figma required.

How to use
this pack

  1. Pick the variant that matches your background. Use the gradient mark on dark surfaces, black on light, white on photos, favicon for anything under 32px.
  2. Copy SVG or download the file. SVGs scale infinitely. For raster needs, download a PNG from the sizes grid below.
  3. Respect the rules from Doc 1. Clear space = 1× rift height. Minimum size 20px. No skewing, stretching, rotating, or recoloring.

/ 01 — Brand Essence

What ShopRift is.

The bridge between storefronts. A migration tool that moves your entire dm2buy store — products, images, collections — through to Shopify in minutes, not days.

ShopRift is the rift between platforms. It pulls your store through a portal and drops it, fully formed, on the other side.

Brand Essence Triangle

/ Mission
Eliminate platform lock-in for Indian D2C sellers.
No seller should be stuck on a platform because migrating is too painful. ShopRift makes leaving instant.
/ Vision
The default migration layer for e-commerce platforms.
Start with dm2buy → Shopify. Expand to every meaningful platform pair. Become the rift between any two stores.
/ Promise
Minutes, not days. Every product. Zero manual work.
If a migration takes longer than your coffee break, we've failed. If a single product is missing, we've failed.

The Audience

Primary: Indian D2C sellers on dm2buy who've scaled past the platform's ceiling — typically 100+ SKUs, ₹5L+ monthly revenue, and are now ready to move to Shopify for the professional storefront, better apps, and international payments.

Secondary: Agencies and freelancers who manage migrations for clients. ShopRift turns a multi-day client project into a 30-minute deliverable.

Future: Sellers migrating between any two platforms — Shopify ↔ WooCommerce, Wix ↔ Shopify, BigCommerce ↔ Shopify.

The One-Line Pitch

Move your entire dm2buy store to Shopify in minutes. Every product, every image, every collection.
/ 02 — Logo System

The mark.

A stylized "S" formed by two angular chevron halves, separated by a sliver of light — the rift. The portal in the name, drawn.

Primary Mark

The full mark with particles. Use this anywhere the logo renders at 64px or larger — app icons, marketing, hero placements, business cards.

Primary Lockup

shoprift

The mark paired with the wordmark — Geist Black 900, lowercase, letter-spacing -0.045em. One word, single weight, super-tight tracking. Use for nav headers, email signatures, decks, and anywhere the brand needs to read as a single word.

/ 04 — Typography

The type system.

Three typefaces with non-overlapping roles. Geist does the heavy lifting. JetBrains Mono signals the technical. Instrument Serif provides editorial accent.

Primary — Geist

Geist · Sans-serif
/ Display + Body
shoprift
Geist is the brand workhorse. It handles the wordmark, all display headlines, UI text, marketing copy, and body paragraphs. Open-source, modern, and built for software — exactly what ShopRift is.
Weights: 300, 400, 500, 600, 700, 800, 900
Default tracking: -0.02em
Source: vercel.com/font/geist

Technical — JetBrains Mono

JetBrains Mono · Monospaced
/ Code + Labels
// READY TO MIGRATE
// Used for: code snippets, status indicators, micro-labels, section tags, and anywhere the interface needs to signal technical precision. Never use for body copy.
Weights: 400, 500, 700
Tracking: +0.05em to +0.2em
Source: jetbrains.com/lp/mono

Accent — Instrument Serif

Instrument Serif · Italic
/ Editorial Accent
the rift
Used exclusively for emphasis — usually paired with the brand gradient. Reserve for single words or phrases inside larger Geist headlines. Never use for headlines on its own, never for body.
Weight: 400 italic only
Use case: emphasis in headlines

/ 05 — Voice & Tone

How ShopRift talks.

Direct. Technical. Slightly understated. Like a senior engineer who's tired of bullshit and just wants to ship the thing.

The Four Voice Pillars

/ 01

Direct

Lead with the answer. Cut the warmup. If migration takes minutes, we say "minutes" — not "blazing-fast performance optimized for modern e-commerce." Short sentences. Concrete numbers. Zero corporate softeners.

/ 02

Technical

We're a tool for people who get it. CSV, API, SKU, listings, collections — we use the real words. We don't dumb things down. We don't over-explain. The audience is sellers who already manage a store; they know the terminology.

/ 03

Understated

We don't shout. We don't use "revolutionary" or "game-changing." The product is impressive enough that hyperbole cheapens it. State the facts, let the user be impressed on their own.

/ 04

Human

We're built by a solo founder, not a corporation. The brand voice should occasionally show that — first-person on the about page, casual on X, available in DMs. Tool-grade product, founder-grade support.

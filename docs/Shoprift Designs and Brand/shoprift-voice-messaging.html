/ 01 — Voice Pillars

The four pillars.

Every word ShopRift puts into the world should pass through these four filters. If a line doesn't feel like all four, rewrite it.

/ 01

Be direct.

Lead with the answer. Cut the warmup. If migration takes minutes, we say "minutes" — not "blazing-fast performance optimized for modern e-commerce." Short sentences. Concrete numbers. Zero corporate softeners.

// Direct 100 products. 3 minutes. Done.
/ 02

Be technical.

We're a tool for people who get it. CSV, API, SKU, listings, collections — we use the real words. We don't dumb things down. We don't over-explain. The audience already manages a store; they know the terminology.

// Technical Imports products, images, and collections directly into Shopify admin.
/ 03

Be understated.

We don't shout. We don't use "revolutionary" or "game-changing." The product is impressive enough that hyperbole cheapens it. State the facts; let the user be impressed on their own.

// Understated Migrate your store. That's the whole thing.
/ 04

Be human.

We're built by a solo founder, not a corporation. The voice should occasionally show that — first-person on the about page, casual on X, available in DMs. Tool-grade product, founder-grade support.

// Human Built by one person. DM me on X if it breaks.

/ 04 — Taglines

Three taglines.

One for clarity, one for brand, one for tight spaces. Pick the one that fits the placement.

/ Primary · Functional
dm2buy to Shopify, in minutes.
The clearest, most useful version. Use for landing page hero, App Store listing, paid ads — anywhere the audience needs to immediately understand what we do.
/ Secondary · Conceptual
The rift between storefronts.
More poetic. Use for brand-focused contexts: about page, decks, social bios, magazine features — anywhere we have permission to be evocative instead of literal.
/ Shortest · Ultra-Compact
Migrate. In minutes.
For very tight spaces: business cards, X bio, email signatures, OG image overlays, favicon hover state.

Same idea, three depths. Use the one that fits the time you have.

Shoprift automates the whole thing: install the app, drop in your dm2buy URL, and your products land directly in Shopify admin. Minutes, not days.
/ Paragraph
Shoprift is a migration tool built for Indian D2C sellers moving from dm2buy to Shopify. When a seller outgrows dm2buy — usually around 100+ products and ₹5L+ in monthly revenue — they want Shopify's app ecosystem, international payments, and professional storefront. But there's no export tool. They either spend days manually re-listing every product, or pay an agency thousands of rupees. Shoprift solves this: install the Shopify app, paste your dm2buy store URL, and every product, image, and collection imports directly into your Shopify admin. The rift between storefronts.
/ 06 — Channels

How to talk on each channel.

Same voice, different volume. X is loud and casual. Landing pages are confident and tight. Emails are warm and direct. Errors are honest.

Landing Page
/ Hero · Marketing
The first impression. Lead with the value prop, back it with proof, end with a low-friction CTA.
  • Headline = the tagline, no decoration
  • Sub-line = one sentence of clarity
  • CTA = a verb, never "Learn More"
  • Numbers everywhere they're real
// Hero example
dm2buy to Shopify,
in minutes.

Products, images, and collections imported directly into Shopify admin.

[ Install on Shopify → ]
X / Twitter
/ Founder · Casual
First-person, loose, occasionally lowercase. This is where the founder shows up. Share progress, screenshots, real numbers, real wins, real bugs.
  • Write in first person ("I built…")
  • Show, don't tell — screenshots, GIFs, numbers
  • Reply to every DM and comment
  • Lowercase is allowed, exclamation marks aren't
// X post example

Email
/ Direct · Warm
Short. Personal. From a real human address, not "noreply@". Sign off with first name. No HTML decoration.
  • Subject lines under 6 words
  • Open with the point, not pleasantries
  • One CTA per email, max
  • Signed: "— Mayank"

Product UI
/ Microcopy · Functional
Verbs over nouns. Numbers over adjectives. Errors that explain. Success states that don't oversell.
  • Buttons = verbs ("Migrate", "Export")
  • Empty states = next action, not encouragement
  • Loading states = real progress, not "Please wait"
  • Errors = what broke + what to do

/ 08 — System Copy

Errors, success, in-progress.

The product talks to users hundreds of times. Each one of these strings should pass the four pillars.

/ Loading
Extracting products… 47 of 213. Hang tight — this'll take about 90 seconds.
/ Success
✓ 213 products imported to your Shopify store. Your products, images, and collections are now live in Shopify admin.
/ Partial Success
208 of 213 products migrated. 5 products skipped because their images couldn't be reached. See the list →
/ Error · URL
Couldn't find that store. Double-check your dm2buy URL. It should look like yourstore.dm2buy.com
/ Error · System
Something broke on our side. Not your fault. Try again in a minute — or DM the founder if it keeps happening.
/ Empty state
No migrations yet. Drop in your dm2buy URL to get started. Takes about 3 minutes.
/ Payment
Payment received. You're on the Pro plan. Migrate as much as you want — no per-product caps.
